# SIRR Governance Constitution / 治理宪法

## Article 1 — Object of governance / 治理对象

The system governs observable semantic acts, distribution mechanisms and commercial incentives. It shall not classify a person as morally good, evil, cognitively inferior, positive or negative.

系统治理可观察的语义行为、传播机制和商业激励，不给人建立善恶、认知高低或正负能量等级。

## Article 2 — Protected adverse expression / 受保护的负面表达

Criticism, whistleblowing, grief, anger, adverse findings, failed experiments and risk warnings shall not be restricted merely because they are negative or uncomfortable.

批评、揭弊、悲伤、愤怒、不利结果、失败实验和风险预警，不得仅因负面或令人不适而受限。

## Article 3 — Intent / 主观意图

Malicious intent shall not be inferred from language style. Deliberate deception requires independent evidence and due process.

不得从语言风格推断恶意；故意欺骗必须有独立证据和正当程序。

## Article 4 — Proportionality / 比例性

Interventions shall begin with context, user control and reversible friction. Distribution restriction, monetization suspension and removal require progressively stronger evidence and human authority.

干预从补语境、用户控制和可逆摩擦开始。去放大、暂停变现和移除需要逐级更强证据与人工权限。

## Article 5 — Due process / 正当程序

Any adverse intervention shall provide notice, reason codes, evidence scope, expiry, appeal and correction.

任何不利措施都必须提供通知、理由码、证据范围、期限、申诉和纠错。

## Article 6 — Children and situational vulnerability / 未成年人和情境脆弱性

Situational vulnerability may justify more explanation, slower defaults and stronger commercial protections. It shall never reduce rights or become a permanent profile.

情境脆弱性只能用于增加解释、降低默认速度和加强商业保护，不得减少权利或成为永久画像。

## Article 7 — Agent authority / Agent权限

Agents may analyze, contextualize and propose. They may not autonomously remove content, impose penalties, contact external parties or create secret cross-user memory.

Agent可以分析、补语境和提出方案，不得自主删除、处罚、联系外部主体或建立秘密跨用户记忆。

## Article 8 — Correction / 纠错

Confirmed error requires restoration, record correction, downstream notification, verified repair and model revision.

错误确认后必须恢复、改记录、通知下游、修复经核实损失并修订模型。
